How it works

  1. Turn it on

    Open PowerToys Settings, select Text Expander and turn on Enable Text Expander.

    PowerToys Settings

    Enable Text Expander On

  2. Add a snippet

    Select New snippet, then give it a trigger, such as !mysig, and the text it should turn into. You can also start from an example.

    PowerToys Settings

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !mysig
    Replacement
    Kind regards,
    Gary
    SaveCancel

  3. Type the trigger

    In email, chat, your browser or most other apps, Text Expander swaps the trigger for your text as you type.

    Any app, such as email

    ToElaine
    Looks great – ship it!

    Kind regards,
    Gary

Make it yours

Short or long

A word, an address or a whole email. By default, Text Expander types short snippets, pastes longer ones and then puts your clipboard back.

Fill in the details

Add the date or time in the format you like, what’s on your clipboard, or something Text Expander asks you each time the snippet runs.

Put the cursor where you need it

Mark a spot with $|$ and the cursor lands there after the snippet expands, ready for you to keep typing.

Undo in one step

Didn’t mean to expand it? Press Ctrl+Z straight away to get back what you typed.

Sync snippets across PCs

Save your snippets in OneDrive or another synced folder to use them across your PCs.

Plain text, full control

Your snippets stay readable and portable in one plain-text file. Edit them in Settings or any text editor—no special editor required.

Variables

A snippet is a trigger, such as !ddate, and the text it turns into, called its replacement. Add a variable to the replacement, and Text Expander fills it in each time the snippet runs. In Settings, Insert variable adds them for you.

  • Date {{date}}

    Today’s date.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !ddate
    Replacement
    {{date}}

    Type !ddate in most apps

    10/18/26

  • Time {{time}}

    The current time.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !ttime
    Replacement
    {{time}}

    Type !ttime in most apps

    09:41 AM

  • Date and time {{datetime}}

    The date and the time together.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !ddt
    Replacement
    {{datetime}}

    Type !ddt in most apps

    10/18/26 09:41 AM

  • ISO date {{isodate}}

    The date as year, month and day. Handy in file names, because it sorts in order.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !isodate
    Replacement
    {{isodate}}

    Type !isodate in most apps

    2026-10-18

  • Custom date format {{date:format}}

    The date your way: dddd is the day of the week, MMMM the month, d the day and yyyy the year.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !longdate
    Replacement
    {{date:dddd, MMMM d, yyyy}}

    Type !longdate in most apps

    Sunday, October 18, 2026

  • Clipboard contents {{clipboard}}

    Whatever you copied last.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !link
    Replacement
    Here’s the link: {{clipboard}}

    Copy a link, then type !link in most apps

    contoso.com/q4-planCtrl+C
    Here’s the link: contoso.com/q4-plan

  • Ask me when it runs {{prompt:question}}

    A small window asks you to type something, with your words as the question.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !hello
    Replacement
    Hi {{prompt:First name}}, thanks for reaching out!

    Type !hello in most apps

    Hi Elaine, thanks for reaching out!
    Text ExpanderFirst nameOK

  • Cursor position $|$

    Where the cursor lands after the snippet expands, so you can fill in a blank.

    Snippet

    Trigger
    !meet
    Replacement
    Are you free to meet on $|$ to go over the Q4 plan?

    Type !meet in most apps

    Are you free to meet on to go over the Q4 plan?

Frequently asked questions

Where are my snippets saved?

In a plain text file called PowerToysTextExpanderSnippets.txt. Unless you sync it, it’s in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\PowerToys\TextExpander. Text Expander reloads it whenever you save, so there’s nothing to restart.

Can I use the same snippets on all my PCs?

Yes. In Text Expander settings, turn on Sync across PCs to keep your snippets in OneDrive. Using another sync app? Choose its folder instead.

Does it work in every app?

In most apps. Snippets don’t expand in apps running as administrator or in standard Windows password boxes. They can still expand in password fields on websites and in some apps.

What if an app doesn’t accept the text?

Some apps block pasting. In Text Expander settings, under Advanced, set Insertion method to Always type.

Why does my trigger fire in the middle of a word?

Start your triggers with a symbol such as !, or turn on Wait for the whole word under Advanced. Text Expander then waits for a space or punctuation before it expands, and leaves the space or punctuation in place.

Can I undo an expansion?

Yes. Right after a snippet expands, press Ctrl+Z to change it back to what you typed. This doesn’t work for snippets that use your clipboard or ask you to type something.

Does Text Expander need administrator rights?

No. It runs as you. To edit snippets, PowerToys needs to run without administrator rights.

Can my organization turn it off?

Yes. Administrators can turn off Text Expander with group policy, using the ConfigureEnabledUtilityTextExpander policy ID. Learn more in the PowerToys group policy documentation.

Where can I ask a question or request a feature?

Open an issue in the PowerToys repository on GitHub.
